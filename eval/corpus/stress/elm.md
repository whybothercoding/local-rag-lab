# Elm office policy

## Hotel cap
The hotel cap is 124 EUR per night.

## Meal allowance
The meal allowance is 125 EUR per day.

## Mileage cap
Mileage claims are capped at 126 km per month.

## Training budget
The training budget is 127 EUR per year.

## Parking cap
The parking subsidy is capped at 128 EUR per month.

## Taxi limit
A single taxi ride may cost at most 129 EUR.
