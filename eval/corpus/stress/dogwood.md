# Dogwood office policy

## Hotel cap
The hotel cap is 118 EUR per night.

## Meal allowance
The meal allowance is 119 EUR per day.

## Mileage cap
Mileage claims are capped at 120 km per month.

## Training budget
The training budget is 121 EUR per year.

## Parking cap
The parking subsidy is capped at 122 EUR per month.

## Taxi limit
A single taxi ride may cost at most 123 EUR.
