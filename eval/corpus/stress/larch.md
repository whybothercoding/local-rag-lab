# Larch office policy

## Hotel cap
The hotel cap is 160 EUR per night.

## Meal allowance
The meal allowance is 161 EUR per day.

## Mileage cap
Mileage claims are capped at 162 km per month.

## Training budget
The training budget is 163 EUR per year.

## Parking cap
The parking subsidy is capped at 164 EUR per month.

## Taxi limit
A single taxi ride may cost at most 165 EUR.
