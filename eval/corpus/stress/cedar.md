# Cedar office policy

## Hotel cap
The hotel cap is 112 EUR per night.

## Meal allowance
The meal allowance is 113 EUR per day.

## Mileage cap
Mileage claims are capped at 114 km per month.

## Training budget
The training budget is 115 EUR per year.

## Parking cap
The parking subsidy is capped at 116 EUR per month.

## Taxi limit
A single taxi ride may cost at most 117 EUR.
