# Hazel office policy

## Hotel cap
The hotel cap is 142 EUR per night.

## Meal allowance
The meal allowance is 143 EUR per day.

## Mileage cap
Mileage claims are capped at 144 km per month.

## Training budget
The training budget is 145 EUR per year.

## Parking cap
The parking subsidy is capped at 146 EUR per month.

## Taxi limit
A single taxi ride may cost at most 147 EUR.
