# Ginkgo office policy

## Hotel cap
The hotel cap is 136 EUR per night.

## Meal allowance
The meal allowance is 137 EUR per day.

## Mileage cap
Mileage claims are capped at 138 km per month.

## Training budget
The training budget is 139 EUR per year.

## Parking cap
The parking subsidy is capped at 140 EUR per month.

## Taxi limit
A single taxi ride may cost at most 141 EUR.
